Articles

Worked case studies and longer-form tutorials. The four case studies below mirror the four endpoint types covered by adabay and are each based on a published phase II/III or phase III trial. The full scripts are shipped in inst/examples/ of the package.

Case studies (one per endpoint type)

Continuous

TRANSFORM-2 esketamine re-design

Esketamine nasal spray vs placebo in treatment-resistant depression; five-look Bayesian GSD on the mean-difference scale; benchmarked against gsbDesign, adaptr and BATSS (Section 5.1).

 Binary

ADRENAL hydrocortisone re-design

Hydrocortisone vs placebo in septic shock; five-look Bayesian GSD on the risk-difference scale; benchmarked against adaptr and BATSS (Section 5.2).

 Count

BNT162b2 vaccine re-design

BNT162b2 mRNA vaccine vs placebo for COVID-19 prevention; five-look Bayesian GSD on the log-rate-ratio scale; benchmarked against BATSS (Section 5.3).

 Time-to-event

CheckMate-141 nivolumab re-design

Nivolumab vs chemotherapy in head and neck cancer; five-look event-driven Bayesian GSD on the log-hazard-ratio scale with Poisson recruitment; no faithful comparator is constructible (Section 5.4).

Vignettes

Longer-form vignettes shipped with the package source under vignettes/:

  • overview. A guided tour of the design specification, simulation entry point, and S3 methods using the ADRENAL re-design.
  • evaluation. Evaluating a Bayesian GSD with conjugate and non-conjugate priors, including the fit_mixture() tail-probability diagnostics.
  • calibration. End-to-end threshold calibration from build_cache() through calibrate_design() for common-across-looks thresholds, plus a stage-specific Haybittle–Peto-style schedule evaluated from the same cache.

Built with knitr. View locally with vignette(package = "adabay") after installing the package.
